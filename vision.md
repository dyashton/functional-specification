# Vision

Turn a molecular objective into an explicit, learnable **Functional Specification** that generation systems can optimize and evaluate—so that **one behavior can be proven through many scaffolds**, without hiding assumptions in ad hoc reward code.

## What this MVP establishes

- Soft neighborhoods in Spec space are structure-diverse and behavior-coherent relative to fingerprints.
- A single Spec admits chemically diversified realizations at stable surrogate behavior.
- Spec conditioning outperforms matched descriptor conditioning on diversity at similar or better control.
- Frozen universal transfer (E9) is **not** established (1/5 tasks); that is a stated scope limit.

## Not claimed here

Environment-derived Interaction Specs (Phase II), wet-lab endpoints, or SOTA de novo generation as the primary contribution.
